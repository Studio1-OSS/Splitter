# Real receipts (CORD v2)

150 photographed restaurant and café receipts from Indonesia, used as the benchmark's real-receipt track.

- **Source:** [CORD v2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/naver-clova-ix/cord-v2) by NAVER Clova AI, from the paper *CORD: A Consolidated Receipt Dataset for Post-OCR Parsing* (Park et al., 2019).
- **Licence:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The photos are resized to at most 1,600 px and re-encoded as JPEG; nothing else is changed. Identifying details were already blurred by the dataset authors.
- **Ground truth:** CORD's human labels for line names, quantities, line totals, tax, service charge, discount and the printed total. Amounts are whole rupiah.
- **Who had what:** generated by `import_cord.py`, not part of CORD. The receipts are real; the notes are synthetic.

## How the 150 were chosen

`python3 benchmark/dataset/import_cord.py` reads CORD in a fixed order (test, validation, then train) and keeps the first 150 receipts whose labels are unambiguous and add up:

- at least 2 line items, every line priced, and unique line names, so a note like "Priya had the Bbq Bento" points at one line;
- no priced sub-items (add-ons) or per-line discounts;
- lines + tax + service − discount equal the printed total.

Each kept receipt records its CORD split and row in `source`, so you can trace it back.
